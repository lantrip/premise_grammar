# Chapter 1

## The Meeting

The atrium of the {Last Library} breathed dust and morning light as {Maya} crossed the tiled floor. The mezzanine loomed with shadows, and the old clock thudded in the rafters. A page slipped a note between catalogs.

"Check the index vault," it read. {Maya} folded it once and watched for {?Kai}. If the rumors were true, the {Night District} would seal again at dusk.

{Maya} is determined to recover the lost index.

## The Interruption

The {Sentinel Guards} entered with the muted stomp of heavy boots. Questions, then warnings. "Restricted sections are off-limits," they said. {Maya} nodded and drifted toward shelf {C-17}.

Behind a row of crumbling folios, her fingers found a hidden map scratched into the wood: a route beneath the {Night District}, toward a vault that hummed like a living thing.
